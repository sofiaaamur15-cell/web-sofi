Sofia Mur is a Madrid-based fashion designer in formation, working through an experimental lens rooted in materiality and form. Her practice engages with philosophical and social frameworks, translating them into concept-driven pieces that explore the relationship between body, structure, and meaning. She develops her work through self-initiated projects and collaborations with emerging creatives, working across styling and creative direction to build cohesive visual narratives. Her approach is driven by research, material experimentation, and a strong emphasis on image-making as a tool for storytelling.
