Sidelines

This project explores the vulnerability focusing on the personal journey of a young girl as she begins to discover her sexuality. It reflects on the feeling of growing up in a world where curiosity and exploration can be met with exposure and uncertainty. Through this work, I wanted to express the balance between developing a sense of identity and navigating the social expectations that often surround young women.
