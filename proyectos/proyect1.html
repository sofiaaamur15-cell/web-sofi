No et fa por aquest ocell?

This collection explores the destruction of identity through language and is inspired by the novel La Plaça del Diamant (The Pigeon Girl), written by Mercè Rodoreda. Rodoreda was a Catalan author who was forced into exile during the dictatorship in order to continue writing in her native language.
